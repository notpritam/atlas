Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Atlas is privacy-first and local by default. Your captures live on your own machine, and enrichment runs on your own AI. We do not run a server that collects your content unless you explicitly point Atlas at one.

In one line: what you capture stays with you. By default nothing leaves your device except to your own local Claude Code for organizing it.

What Atlas captures

Only what you deliberately save: screenshots and region grabs, highlighted text, bookmarks/links, tweets, and quick notes — together with basic page metadata (title, URL, favicon, timestamp) for the item you save.

Where your data is stored

  • Locally on your device — captures are stored in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB). They are not uploaded anywhere by default.
  • Your own AI, on your machine — to organize/tag/summarize a capture, Atlas sends it to your own local Claude Code companion at http://127.0.0.1. This runs on your computer; it is not our server.
  • Optional self-hosted backend — if you configure a backend URL and token (e.g. your own atlas.notpritam.in instance), captures are sent there instead. That server is operated by you (or whoever you point to), under your control.

Permissions, and why

  • activeTab, scripting, tabs — to screenshot the current page and read the text/selection you choose to save, only when you trigger a capture.
  • <all_urls> host access — so you can capture from any page you're on. Atlas does not read pages in the background; it acts only when you invoke it.
  • http://localhost / 127.0.0.1 — to talk to your own local AI companion for enrichment.
  • storage — to keep your captures and settings locally.
  • contextMenus, alarms — the right-click "Save to Atlas" menu and periodic local enrichment of pending items.

What we do NOT do

  • No analytics, tracking, ads, or fingerprinting.
  • No selling or sharing of your data — ever.
  • No account required. No background collection of your browsing.
  • No transmission of your captures to us. (There is no "us" server in the default setup.)

Third parties

None by default. Enrichment uses your own local Claude Code. If you opt into a hosted backend, your data goes only to the server you configured.

Deleting your data

Delete individual captures in the extension, or remove the extension to clear its local storage. If you use a self-hosted backend, delete items there directly.

Children

Atlas is a productivity tool not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We'll update this page and the "last updated" date when the policy changes.

Contact
