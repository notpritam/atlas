Privacy & data.

Updated 10 September 2026

FoundKeep keeps the items you explicitly save in one private collection. The browser extension can keep a local library and sync new captures after you connect it. The iPhone app saves the items you send to FoundKeep from the iOS Share menu directly to your account.

What you save

FoundKeep captures screenshots, highlighted text, bookmarks, images, posts on X, notes, videos, audio, documents and other files when you use its capture or share actions. Saving a page can collect a readable copy of its useful text; headings; visited and canonical URLs; title and description; publisher and author; published and modified dates; language; lead image and favicon; capture and extraction timestamps; the capture method; extractor version; extraction status; and a content fingerprint. A saved link or image also records its target URL. A shared file keeps its original filename, declared media type, byte size and available source context. Sites and sharing apps do not always provide every field.

Your local library

Captures are stored in IndexedDB in the browser profile where FoundKeep is installed. Extension connection settings and short-lived caches of your capture preferences and FoundKeep’s data-only runtime policy are stored in browser extension storage. You can save, browse and search captures without an account. Without a connected account, captures stay in this local library. Existing local captures are not uploaded automatically when you connect.

Your iPhone app

The iPhone app receives content only when you choose FoundKeep in an iOS Share menu or create a note inside the app. It does not monitor other apps, your browsing history, taps, messages or clipboard. Safari can provide readable page text and source metadata to the Share Extension for the page you explicitly share. Photos, Files and other apps can provide the selected image, video, audio, document, link or text.

The app stores its revocable device credential in the iOS Keychain. The app and Share Extension use a private App Group container for a bounded data-only policy and an upload queue. If the network is unavailable, the selected item remains in that protected queue and retries after FoundKeep is opened or the Share Extension is used again. Each shared file is limited to 50 MiB, and the account-wide limits below still apply.

Capture-ready notifications are optional and off until you enable them in the app. When enabled, FoundKeep stores the device's Expo push token with that revocable iPhone connection and sends a generic processing-status alert through Expo and Apple Push Notification service. The alert contains a FoundKeep capture identifier and app route so tapping it can open that item; it does not include the saved title, text, source URL, file, email address or account name. Turning alerts off, signing out, revoking the iPhone connection or deleting the account removes the token from FoundKeep.

FoundKeep uses Expo Application Services to deliver compatible app updates over HTTPS. For update delivery and reliability, Expo may receive a randomized update token, operating-system information, IP address, and crash or diagnostic information. This data is not sent to FoundKeep for advertising and is not linked to your FoundKeep account. See Expo’s privacy policy.

Your optional cloud account

Creating an account stores your name and email address on FoundKeep’s server. If you use a password, your password and one-time recovery code are stored as hashes, rather than readable values. Browser login uses an HTTP-only session cookie. The browser extension and iPhone app receive separate revocable connection credentials stored in their protected platform storage.

When social sign-in is available and you choose it, Supabase Auth processes the identity supplied by your selected provider for FoundKeep. FoundKeep receives your verified email, display name and account identifier; it does not request access to your emails, repositories or posts. Supabase credentials and provider session tokens are handled by FoundKeep’s backend and are not embedded in the app, extension or website. Your saved collection remains on FoundKeep. See Supabase’s privacy policy and your sign-in provider’s privacy policy.

Sign-in providers using the same verified email can connect to one FoundKeep account. FoundKeep stores each linked identity identifier and the email verified when it was linked. A password-only account requires its current password before the first provider is connected. Different addresses, including Apple private relay addresses, are kept separate.

After you connect a browser from the dashboard, FoundKeep stores a browser-and-operating-system label and last-seen time so you can recognize and revoke that connection. New captures upload when automatic sync is enabled. Uploaded content can include screenshots and images, selected or readable page text, notes, the source record described above and the processing choices active when you saved it. Pending uploads remain in the browser and retry when a connection is available. You can explicitly import older local captures from the extension. Cloud captures and preferences are scoped to your account.

The web dashboard uses your account to retrieve saved captures and images. It does not copy your browser’s local library unless you choose to import it. Cloud account limits are 1,000 captures and 200 MB; your account settings show current usage.

Cloud processing

FoundKeep’s server can process cloud captures to extract text from images and derive summaries and tags. You can turn OCR, summaries and automatic tags on or off in your capture settings. The choices active when a capture is saved travel with that capture. Processing runs on stored content; it does not execute commands contained in captures or browse their source pages.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

FoundKeep uses information received through Chrome extension permissions only to provide and improve its single purpose: saving content you choose into your private local or connected library. FoundKeep does not sell this data, use it for advertising or credit decisions, or transfer it except where needed to provide FoundKeep, protect the service, comply with law, or complete a merger or acquisition with prior consent where required. People do not read private capture content unless you explicitly ask for support involving specific content, access is necessary for security, or access is required by law. FoundKeep’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Account access and recovery

For password accounts, your recovery code is shown once when you create your account, recover access or change your password. Keep it somewhere private: someone with your email and recovery code can reset your password. FoundKeep does not send password-reset emails. Social-only accounts use their sign-in provider to recover access and verify account deletion.

Account settings let you revoke individual connected browsers and devices. Recovery and password changes revoke existing connections and other sessions. Revocation stops cloud access; it does not remotely erase captures already saved locally or waiting in a device queue. Logging out of the website does not disconnect extensions or the iPhone app.

Your account stores one capture-preferences document used by connected browsers. It controls available capture methods, page extraction, note source attachment, popup layout, automatic sync and organization. The dashboard can ask an installed FoundKeep extension to refresh after you save changes; the website does not receive the extension credential. FoundKeep also publishes validated data-only browser and mobile policies for feature availability, bounded limits, notices and minimum supported versions. These policies cannot add executable code, permissions or new service origins. App interface and copy updates can arrive through EAS Update for the installed native runtime; native permissions, extensions and security capabilities require an App Store update.

Browser permissions

  • Active page and scripting: support capturing page content, selecting regions and showing FoundKeep’s on-page capture controls after an explicit action. Browser restrictions can prevent capture on protected pages.
  • Image-host access: when you choose Save image, FoundKeep asks for optional access to that image’s public site so it can retrieve the selected original file. It does not retain permanent access to unrelated sites.
  • Storage: keeps extension settings locally; the capture library uses IndexedDB.
  • Context menus and alarms: provide right-click capture actions and scheduled extension work, including retrying cloud uploads.
  • Connection access: supports account pairing and authenticated cloud sync with FoundKeep.

FoundKeep temporarily uses the request IP address in an in-memory rate-limit key to prevent abuse of account and upload endpoints. The key expires with its short rate-limit window and is not added to your capture library.

Deleting saved items

Delete a capture in the web dashboard or iPhone app to remove it from your cloud library and delete its uploaded file, if any. Delete it in the extension’s local library to remove that local copy. These collections are separate; deleting one does not erase the other. Removing the extension also removes its local extension data.

Export and account deletion

Download your cloud library as a JSON export from account settings. Exports include saved text, source records, image data and capture preferences, and may contain private information. Deleting your account removes its active cloud captures, uploaded files, preferences and credentials and revokes its sessions and device connections. Local copies on your devices remain until you delete them there.

For a linked social account, FoundKeep also requests deletion of all its linked Supabase identities and retries if that service is unavailable. A minimal identity identifier is retained while cleanup is pending, and a security marker is retained for up to 24 hours to prevent an unfinished sign-in from recreating the deleted account. Temporary sign-in records expire within 10 minutes and are cleared by the backend. Deleting FoundKeep does not delete your Google, Apple, GitHub or X account.

Operational backups may retain earlier copies until those backups are replaced. Account deletion does not erase files you previously exported.

Questions and source code

Visit FoundKeep Support for installation, connection, capture, sync and account recovery help. Implementation details are available in the source repository. Avoid posting private captures, passwords, recovery codes or connection credentials in public issues.
