How it works

Three moving parts. All yours.

01

The extension captures

A tiny Chrome extension posts what you clip to your backend over an authenticated link.

02

Your box stores it

A self-hosted service on your own machine keeps the originals and a full-text index.

03

Your agent enriches

Your AI agent drains the queue — OCR, tags, summaries, links — and surfaces it in your library.

Self-hosted by design

Your second brain lives on your hardware.

No accounts, no third-party cloud, no one mining your reading history. The backend is a single small service you run yourself; the code is open. Point the extension at your own domain and you're done.
