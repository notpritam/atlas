Atlas

Redeem your invite

Enter your invite code to get your account token — for the extension and your agent.

  1. Open the Atlas extension → Settings ⚙.
  2. Under Agent control relay, set URL to wss://atlas.notpritam.in/agent and paste the token.
  3. Save — it should show “Connected to relay”.
  4. Give the same token to your agent (ATLAS_RELAY_URL + ATLAS_TOKEN).
