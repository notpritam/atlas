# Public collections

Visitors discover and read curated links, notes, and ideas, then follow, share, or contribute according to real collection rules. Inherit the white, azure, sky, mint, and ink web palette, Clarity City headings, Geist body text, and canonical FoundKeep mark.

Lead with the title, curator identity, real counts, actions, and a topic illustration. Use a ruled reading list with an optional card grid. Provenance and context stay visible; long notes use native details. Desktop has a 280px contribution/rules column, tablet 250px; below 760px content stacks and the contribution action moves beside the introduction. Explore uses three/two/one card columns.

Search applies before pagination and shares the permitted-entry predicate with counts and available tags. Preserve pending/private boundaries, server-rendered reading, native GET search, labeled 48px controls, visible focus, useful empty states, real pending feedback, reduced motion, and account-bound mutations. Clipboard denial exposes a selectable link. Refresh preserves loaded pages and stays tied to the initial account.

Topic illustrations are first-party SVGs derived from title/tags; custom-cover uploads are not introduced. Demo content is labeled and confined to dev, with credentials outside the checkout. Evidence is in `docs/ux/public-collections/`; repeatable setup is in `docs/DEV_DEMO.md`.
